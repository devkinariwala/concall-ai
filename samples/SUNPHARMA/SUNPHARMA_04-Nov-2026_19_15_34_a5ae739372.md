## Notable Analyst Q&A

### Dr. Kunal Dhamesha (Macquarie Capital) - *(Page 7)*
- **Q:** Analyst inquired about the drivers behind the growth slowdown of Ilumya in the US, corrective actions taken, and the timeline for recovery.
- **A:** Management stated they identified key issues, put interventions in place, and combined with the psoriatic arthritis launch, expect Ilumya to return to typical growth patterns over the next few quarters.
  > "First one on the slowdown in growth for Ilumya in US Rick, can you highlight what is driving that?"
  > "Yes, I don't want to get into the -- all the details, but we have identified a few key things that have led to the slowdown."
  > "Over the next few quarters."

### Vishal Manchanda (Systematix) - *(Page 14)*
- **Q:** Did the Indian drug regulator ask Sun Pharma to discontinue ophthalmic production at its Assam plant, and will it impact numbers?
