## Notable Analyst Q&A

### Chandra Srikanth (Moneycontrol) - *(Page 9)*
- **Q:** Despite a healthy TCV, why did management narrow the guidance band, and what opportunities and challenges does Infosys see in securing enterprise systems against AI-driven cyber attacks?
- **A:** Management explained that guidance reflects strong AI growth and mega deals, offset by AI productivity impacts, competitive intensity, changing offshore ratios, and selective contract choices based on economic thresholds.
  > "Despite a healthy TCV, why did you sort of narrow the guidance band?"

### Bryan Bergin (TD Cowen) - *(Page 32)*
- **Q:** What changed most in the growth guidance revision versus last quarter, and what are the largest drivers?
- **A:** Management cited pricing pressure from competitive intensity, softer than expected volume growth, and slower discretionary spend in Retail and Hi-Tech sectors.
  > "If you look at what we are seeing today is a pricing pressure on the back of competitive intensity, which has increased in the last couple of quarters."
  > "We saw softer than expected revenue growth or a volume growth in the quarter. So that was the second reason. And slower discretionary spend in some of the sectors, especially the Retail and HiTech that we called out."
